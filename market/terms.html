Market terms and privacy

Card Deals does not hold money, take a fee, or guarantee a trade. The asking price and any offer are between the buyer and the seller. Payment is made directly by Interac e-Transfer or PayPal. For buyer protection, use PayPal Goods and Services.

A listing, offer, report, rating, display name, and payment handle are stored in the database the site owner connects. Payment handles are shown only to the buyer of an accepted offer, and only while that payment is still open. Reports can be filed, and are not shown back in the market.

A photo is uploaded only if the seller checks the consent box. An uploaded photo can be seen with the listing. If the box is not checked, the photo stays on the device and is not sent.

Sign-in uses an email link. The email address is used to open the account and is not turned into a public name. The display name is whatever the person types.

Prices shown as estimates or stored sold figures come from data already on the device. They are not a promise of what the card will sell for. A rating is a number from 1 to 5 entered by someone who finished that trade. The site does not invent ratings, users, or prices.
